{\Large{Disparities in Maternal Nutrition among Indian Social Groups: The Roles of Fertility Patterns and Household Wealth} }

% \subsection*{\textbf{Keywords:} Maternal nutrition, caste, pregnancy, decomposition}

{\setstretch{0.25}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Twenty-two percent of Indian women begin pregnancy underweight.
    \item Pregnant Adivasi and Dalit women are most likely to be underweight.
    \item Fertility patterns explain little of the gaps across social groups.
    \item Differences in household wealth \emph{do} explain gaps across social groups.
\end{itemize}
